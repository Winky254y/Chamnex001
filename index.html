FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions

Find answers to your most asked questions about our services

How much does a solar installation cost?

+

Solar installation costs vary based on your energy needs, property size, and system capacity. We offer free consultations and site visits to provide accurate quotes. On average, residential systems range from KES 300,000 to KES 1,500,000 depending on capacity and components.

What's your warranty on installations?

+

We provide comprehensive warranties on all our installations. Solar panels come with 25-year manufacturer warranties, inverters have 10-year warranties, and our workmanship is guaranteed for 5 years. We stand behind our work 100%.

Do you offer emergency services?

+

Yes! We provide 24/7 emergency electrical services. Whether it's a power outage, electrical fault, or urgent repair, call us anytime. We respond quickly to emergency calls and have experienced technicians ready to help you.

How long does installation take?

+

Installation duration varies by project scope. Residential lighting projects typically take 1-3 days, solar installations 2-5 days, and commercial projects depend on complexity. We provide timeline estimates during your initial consultation.

Do you provide financing options?

+

Yes! We work with several financing partners to make solar energy and electrical upgrades affordable. We can arrange payment plans and financing options. Ask about our flexible payment plans during consultation.

Are you licensed and insured?

+

Absolutely! All our electricians are fully licensed by the Energy and Petroleum Regulatory Authority (EPRA). We maintain comprehensive liability and indemnity insurance for all our work. Your safety and peace of mind are our top priorities.
