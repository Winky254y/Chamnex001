FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions

Find answers to your most asked questions about our services

☀️

How much does a solar installation cost?

+

Solar installation costs vary based on your energy needs, property size, and system capacity. We offer free consultations and site visits to provide accurate quotes. On average, residential systems range from KES 300,000 to KES 1,500,000 depending on capacity and components.

✅

What's your warranty on installations?

+

We provide comprehensive warranties on all our installations. Solar panels come with 25-year manufacturer warranties, inverters have 10-year warranties, and our workmanship is guaranteed for 5 years. We stand behind our work 100%.

🚨

Do you offer emergency services?

+

Yes! We provide 24/7 emergency electrical services. Whether it's a power outage, electrical fault, or urgent repair, call us anytime. We respond quickly to emergency calls and have experienced technicians ready to help you.

⏱️

How long does installation take?

+

Installation duration varies by project scope. Residential lighting projects typically take 1-3 days, solar installations 2-5 days, and commercial projects depend on complexity. We provide timeline estimates during your initial consultation.

💳

Do you provide financing options?

+

Yes! We work with several financing partners to make solar energy and electrical upgrades affordable. We can arrange payment plans and financing options. Ask about our flexible payment plans during consultation.

📜

Are you licensed and insured?

+

Absolutely! All our electricians are fully licensed by the Energy and Petroleum Regulatory Authority (EPRA). We maintain comprehensive liability and indemnity insurance for all our work. Your safety and peace of mind are our top priorities.

🌧️

Do solar panels work in rainy weather?

+

Solar panels still generate electricity on cloudy and rainy days, though at reduced efficiency. Modern panels can generate 10-25% of their rated capacity even in poor weather. Kenya's climate is ideal for solar energy year-round. We design systems accounting for seasonal variations.

🔋

How long do solar batteries last?

+

High-quality lithium batteries typically last 10-15 years with proper maintenance. We use premium brands with warranties covering this period. Lifepo4 batteries we install can even last 20+ years. Regular maintenance ensures optimal performance and longevity.

⚡

What causes frequent power outages in homes?

+

Common causes include overloaded circuits, faulty wiring, aging electrical panels, and power distribution issues. Our electricians can diagnose the root cause and recommend solutions. Many issues can be prevented with proper installation and regular maintenance checks.

💬

How can I get a free quotation?

+

It's simple! Call us at 0722 905 171, WhatsApp us, or fill out our contact form on this website. We'll schedule a free site visit where our experts assess your needs and provide a detailed, obligation-free quotation. No hidden costs!

📊

How much money will I save with solar?

+

Savings depend on your current electricity consumption and system size. Most customers save 60-90% on monthly electricity bills. A typical 5kW system saves customers KES 5,000-15,000 per month. Your investment pays for itself in 3-5 years, then it's free electricity!

🏠

Do I need a home rewiring?

+

Not always. We assess your home's electrical system and recommend upgrades only if necessary. If your home is over 30 years old, has outdated wiring, or you're adding new circuits, rewiring might be beneficial. We'll advise you during our consultation.
